Before you book

Answers we give every week.

What do I need to collect?

A valid licence held for the minimum period on the vehicle page, a second form of ID, and a credit card in the hirer’s name. International licences require an English translation or IDP where the law asks for one.

Can someone else drive?

Yes. Additional drivers must meet the same age and licence rules and be named before the car leaves. Fee: $18 / day, capped at $90.

What if my flight is late?

Send the number. We track it. International arrivals include 45 minutes of wait. After that, waiting is billed at the chauffeur hourly rate if a driver is assigned.

What cover is included?

Comprehensive motor insurance with a stated excess. Excess amounts are confirmed on the reservation. Reduction products are available except on some performance cars.

Track days?

Not permitted. Timed events, launches, and closed-circuit use void cover. We will not release a performance car if we believe that is the plan.

What happens if the car is damaged?

Photograph, call the visor number, do not drive if unsafe. We arrange recovery and a replacement when one is available. Excess applies unless a third party is identified and accepted by insurers.

How do I pay?

Credit card at confirmation. A security hold is placed at delivery on performance cars. Corporate accounts invoice monthly on Net 15.

Cancellation?

Full refund 72 hours before start for sedans and SUVs. 7 days for performance. Inside that window, 50% unless we re-let the dates.

Fuel?

Full to full. If you return short, we refuel at pump plus $25. We do not sell prepaid tanks.
