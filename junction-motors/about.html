Why we exist

Junction started when our founder kept being handed cars that looked right in the photograph and tired in person. We bought the first eight cars ourselves. We still own every vehicle on the site. Nothing is brokered from a third-party lot the morning of your hire.

Workshops sit next to the hubs. Technicians are on payroll. Concierge is staffed at night because flights do not respect office hours. The standard is simple: the car should still feel special on the hundredth mile.
